Research

  1. 2026paper

    Exposure Is Not Commitment: Double-Stratified Evaluation of Latent Monitors for Tool-Using Agents

    Rishabh Sinha (sole author)

    Agents in the Wild @ NeurIPS 2026, accepted poster

    arXiv 2610.37679code & artifacts

    TL;DRSeparates exposure to an injection from commitment to act on it, in both the evaluation and the training population. Probes trained on the naive contrast mostly learn exposure; retrained on exposed cases only, they predict the agent’s own unsafe tool call up to two steps early, with commitment AUROC 0.85 to 0.91 across three models.

    Simon Sure, Sarthak Munshi, Rishabh Sinha, Zhun Wang, Dawn Song
