Mini projects from the exploration phase of Neel Nanda's MATS 12 stream, October 2026.

I banned board-word tokens from gpt-oss-120b's reasoning. It respelled the words.

On 200 new Codenames boards, full reasoning improved the spymaster's score from 1.29 to 2.00 team words per clue.

Inkling plans to admit it's an AI. Force the first words of its reply, and it may claim to be human.

Ethan Hadley found a customer-support case where Inkling's written reasoning says to admit it's an AI, but its reply sometimes claims to be from a real person.

Thought Branches' transplant curves jump, but the matched control complicates the story

I reran Thought Branches' hint-transplant experiment on its released model and data, adding a control: no-hint chains of thought (CoTs) that reached the same wrong answer.

Can a model game an evaluation without saying it noticed? I couldn't get the decisive runs

I tried to test whether saying "this is a honeypot" is necessary for a model to game an evaluation. Neither route gave me enough suitable runs, so the central question remains unanswered.

A judge rated the same transcript higher when told it was its own

I wanted to test whether an AI judge treats its own agent transcripts differently. The actual author made no detectable difference to its ratings.
