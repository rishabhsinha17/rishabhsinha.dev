  3. 03 Thought Branches with a matched control Hint transplant curves jump at single sentences, and the late hint effect may come from how the released traces were selected. CoT faithfulness
  4. 04 Gaming an evaluation without saying so No route produced runs to test silent gaming, but blocking talk of a detector undid much of a disguised copy. Eval awareness
  5. 05 Do AI judges recognise their own work? A “your own” label raised ratings of identical transcripts, while the true author made no detectable difference. Monitoring
